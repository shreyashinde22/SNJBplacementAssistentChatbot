====================================================================
SNJB'S LATE SAU. KANTABAI BHAVARLALJI JAIN COLLEGE OF ENGINEERING
CAMPUS RECRUITMENT STAGES & EVALUATION FLOW
====================================================================

1. STAGE 1: REGISTRATION & PRE-ELIGIBILITY CHECK
- The T&P Cell publishes company drive circulars on official communication channels.
- Students must submit their confirmation before the deadline.
- Student credentials are verified against departmental records.

2. STAGE 2: PRE-PLACEMENT TALK (PPT)
- Visiting company representatives present their organizational vision, job roles, locations, package structures, and career growth.
- Mandatory attendance for registered candidates.

3. STAGE 3: ONLINE / OFFLINE APTITUDE & TECHNICAL TEST
- Sections typically cover Quantitative Aptitude, Logical Reasoning, Verbal Ability, Domain Technical MCQs, and Coding Problems.
- Shortlisted candidates advance to Stage 4.

4. STAGE 4: GROUP DISCUSSION (GD) / TECHNICAL CODING ROUND
- GD tests communication skills, leadership, logical articulation, and listening skills.
- Technical coding round evaluates problem-solving and algorithmic logic.

5. STAGE 5: TECHNICAL & HR INTERVIEWS
- Technical Interviewers assess core engineering fundamentals, project implementations, and problem-solving approach.
- HR Interviewers evaluate organizational fit, adaptability, communication skills, and willingness to relocate.

6. STAGE 6: RESULT ANNOUNCEMENT & OFFER LETTER ISSUANCE
- Official results are released exclusively through the Head of T&P Cell.
- Selected candidates receive offer letters/intent letters subject to meeting final semester graduation criteria.
