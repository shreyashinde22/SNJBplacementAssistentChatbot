TRAINING & PLACEMENT CELL - OFFICIAL POLICY GUIDELINES (DOCUMENT REF: SNJB-TP-2025-01)
====================================================================

1. OBJECTIVE
The Training and Placement (T&P) Cell at SNJB College of Engineering aims to provide guidance, skill development, and employment opportunities to eligible engineering students across all departments (Computer Engineering, AI&DS, E&TC, Mechanical, Civil, etc.).

2. GENERAL ELIGIBILITY & REGISTRATION RULES
- Registration with the T&P Cell is mandatory for participating in any campus recruitment drive.
- Students must maintain at least 75% attendance in T&P training sessions, mock interviews, and skill building workshops to remain eligible for campus drives.
- Academic eligibility (percentage/CGPA, maximum active backlogs, gap years) is governed by individual recruiting organizations.
- Candidates must provide genuine academic, personal, and project records. False information results in permanent debarment from placement activities.

3. CODE OF CONDUCT DURING RECRUITMENT DRIVES
- Formal dress code is mandatory for all pre-placement talks, aptitude tests, group discussions, and interview rounds.
- Candidates must arrive at least 15 minutes prior to the scheduled drive time with all required document copies.
- Absence from a drive after registering without prior written permission from the Placement Officer may result in suspension from subsequent placement opportunities.

4. ONE STUDENT ONE OFFER POLICY (GENERAL RULE)
- Once a student receives a confirmed job offer through the college campus drive, they are deemed "Placed" and are generally not eligible to participate in further drives for similar pay bands.
- Exception: A placed student may participate in a "Dream Offer" drive if the package offered exceeds their current offer by at least 50% or as defined by the annual T&P policy.

5. VERIFICATION & DISCLAIMER
- All company drive dates, eligibility criteria, CTC packages, and bond terms announced in official drive circulars supersede general policy.
- For verified information on upcoming company visits, contact the Head of Training & Placement Cell, SNJB COE, Chandwad.
